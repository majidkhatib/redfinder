ABI Red Item Detector

Listens to your PC's sound output and alerts when it hears an Arena Breakout: Infinite merchant sound that belongs to a red item. Sounds and items from abibuilder.com/merchant. Everything runs locally in your browser.

Not listening

1. Audio source

Chrome / Edge / Brave: in the share dialog choose the Entire screen tab (not a window: a window has no sound) and tick Share system audio.

Firefox can't capture system audio directly. Use an input device below.

For an input device, pick a loopback device that carries your game audio: Windows Stereo Mix (enable it in Sound settings → Recording) or CABLE Output from VB-Audio Virtual Cable.
